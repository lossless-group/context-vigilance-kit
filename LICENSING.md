## On sharing your context-v/

We expect most organizations to keep their `context-v/` content proprietary. It's often full of client names, strategy, and decisions made in private, and that's fine.

We encourage sharing what you can, though. This practice got better because we wrote our specs, explorations, and post-mortems in public. Publishing a generic version of a doc, or sending an improvement back to the kit, helps the next team.
