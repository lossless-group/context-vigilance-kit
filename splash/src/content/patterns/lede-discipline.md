## What it is

`lede` is the line under a title in every list view, card, and share preview. It says why you'd care, not what the thing is (`title` does that).

## Why

We once briefed a sweep to write ledes "about three lines long." It produced dozens of good paragraphs, every one clipped mid-sentence in a share card. Prose limits don't constrain writers, human or agent. A number does.

We also tried generating ledes from document bodies. The results were horizontal rules, sentences cut off at "e.g.", and lines ending in a colon. A lede needs someone who has read the doc and judged what's interesting.

## How

- 140 characters max; aim for 90 to 130.
- If it wants to grow, start a *Why care?* section under the title instead.
- A stub gets no lede. An empty lede on an empty doc is honest.
