## What it is

Every doc in `context-v/` has three audiences: its author, a future agent session with no memory of today, and an outside reader arriving from a link.

## Why

The agent and the stranger have the same problem: no context. A doc that serves the stranger serves the agent.

## How

- Lead with why, in a first paragraph an outsider can follow. Technical depth comes later.
- Name things plainly. Internal shorthand is the first thing both readers trip on.
- Prefer genericizing a sensitive specific over hiding a whole doc. Keep `publish: false` for what genuinely can't be shared.
